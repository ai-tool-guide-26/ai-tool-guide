Three checks before you pay

  1. Read the free-tier limits. Generation caps, storage, watermarks and commercial rights vary.
  2. Find the auto-renew terms. Most free trials convert to paid automatically.
  3. Check the date on the comparison. AI pricing and affiliate terms change every few months.

Program notes (for publishers)

Most recurring AI programs pay 20–30% for 12 months. Murf runs up to 24 months, and AdCreative.ai and KrispCall advertise lifetime commissions. Semrush pays $100–$300 per sale with a 120-day cookie and suggests at least 1,000 monthly visits for content sites. Verify all figures on the official program page before you publish them.

FAQ

Do ChatGPT or Claude pay affiliate commissions?

No cash affiliate program for individuals has been confirmed for the major model vendors. Commissions sit with adjacent tools.

Which AI affiliates pay recurring commissions?

Most pay 20–30% for 12 months. Murf (24 months), AdCreative.ai and KrispCall (lifetime) are the longer exceptions. Terms change often.

How long until the first payout?

Approval, a 30–90 day hold, and a minimum threshold are normal. Six months from click to cash is common.
